Get help

Questions, bug reports or account deletion requests: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository, or use the support contact on the App Store listing. We usually reply within a few days.

Report an issue

Delete your account or data

In the app: Settings › Delete Account removes you from your team (or deletes the team if you are its only member), signs you out and clears your device. Team admins can delete the whole team under Settings › Delete Team. You can also revoke Squadly's access under Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple on your device.
